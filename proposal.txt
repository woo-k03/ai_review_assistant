Project Summary: Continuing Our Lab's Work on Protein Folding

We want to keep doing research on protein folding, which is what our lab has been doing for the
last six years under our current NIH R01 grant. This pilot grant would basically let us keep the
lab running smoothly while we wait to hear back on our R01 renewal, since our current funding runs
out soon and this would cover the gap.

We plan to use the same computational modeling techniques we've always used, just applied to a
couple of new proteins. It should work fine since it's basically the same method.

Budget:
- New lab server (~$4,500)
- PI summer salary support: $8,000
- Travel to two conferences to present results: $3,200
- Remaining funds for supplies

We're confident this will go well since we've done similar work before, though we haven't decided
exactly which proteins we'll look at yet or how we'll know if it worked.
