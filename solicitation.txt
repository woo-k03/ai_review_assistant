Coastal Innovation Pilot Grant Program

PURPOSE: Fund early-stage, genuinely novel research pilots ($25,000, one year) that are not
currently supported by any other funding source. Priority given to projects proposing a new
technique or approach not previously used by the applicant's lab.

ELIGIBILITY: Full-time faculty at an accredited institution. This grant may NOT be used to bridge
funding between existing grants, and may NOT support work that substantially overlaps with the
applicant's current funded research.

ALLOWABLE COSTS: laboratory supplies; small equipment under $3,000; part-time research assistant
salary.

NOT PERMITTED: PI salary, travel, indirect/overhead costs, equipment over $3,000, tuition.

APPLICATION REQUIREMENTS: A 2-page project summary describing (1) the innovative approach, (2) how
it differs from the applicant's existing funded work, (3) a clear plan to assess success, and (4)
investigator qualifications relevant to the proposed technique.

REVIEW CRITERIA: proposals are scored on (a) fit with program objectives, (b) genuine innovation,
(c) clarity and quality of writing, (d) investigator/team capability to execute the work.
